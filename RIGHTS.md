# Rights and reuse

This repository contains research documentation created for Graham Newman's doctoral research and the associated Turin 2026 conference paper.

## Research documentation

Unless and until an explicit licence is added, no open licence is asserted for the author's original research documentation or datasets. This includes tables and figures. All rights are reserved.

## Archival material

Archival records referenced by this repository remain subject to the rights, access and reproduction conditions of their holding institutions and other rights holders. Material associated with RCA Special Collections and the Victoria and Albert Museum must not be assumed to be openly reusable merely because a citation or identifier appears here. The same applies to transcriptions and research notes.

This repository is designed to preserve provenance and analytical transparency. It does not transfer copyright or reproduction rights in underlying archival objects.

## Screenshots and interface captures

UAT screenshots may contain quotations, catalogue metadata or reproductions derived from archival sources. Public redistribution should therefore be checked against the relevant source and institutional rights conditions before upload.

## Citation is not permission

Persistent identifiers, citations and source references establish provenance. They do not themselves grant permission to reproduce the source material.
